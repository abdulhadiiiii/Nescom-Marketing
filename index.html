BEHIND EVERY INSTALLATION

ENGINEERED AS ONE COMPLETE SYSTEM

Every Nescom elevator is built with carefully selected components that work together for dependable performance, lasting reliability and a smooth journey.

Traction Machine & Motor

The drive unit that raises and lowers the cabin with precise torque and speed control.

Guide Rails

Steel rails that keep the cabin and counterweight perfectly aligned along the shaft.

Cabin Enclosure

The passenger or load compartment finished, sized and rated to the project's spec.

Control Panel

Governs floor selection, ride speed, door timing and real-time system diagnostics.

Safety Governor

Continuously monitors cabin speed and triggers emergency braking if limits are exceeded.

Counterweight

Balances the cabin's load against the motor, reducing energy use and mechanical strain.

Door Operator

Controls smooth, synchronized opening and closing, with obstruction sensing built in.

Hoistway & Shaft

The vertical structure that houses and protects the entire elevator system, floor to floor.
