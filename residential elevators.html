Residential Elevators

Elegant, space-saving, and comfortable home lifting solutions tailored for luxury villas and bungalows.

Overview

NESCOM Marketing provides safe, reliable, and stylish residential elevators designed for smooth and comfortable movement between floors.
We offer a wide range of affordable and modern solutions, including compact, outdoor, glass, MRL, traction, Cargo, and passenger elevators. With customized designs, quality installation, and cost-effective pricing, we deliver residential lift solutions tailored to your home and specific requirements.

Key Features

Space-Saving Design

Compact shaft dimensions with minimum pit and overhead height requirements, perfect for existing or new homes.

Machine-Room-Less (MRL)

Advanced MRL gearless technology saves valuable home square footage and operates with high efficiency.

Uncompromised Safety

Integrated with Automatic Rescue Devices (ARD), emergency battery backup, and soft touch safety gear.
